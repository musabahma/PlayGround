How to play

A rival clan has claimed the valley. Build wisely, then take their stronghold.

SELECT

Click a unit or drag a box around several units.

MOVE

Right-click any open ground to move selected units.

GATHER

Select a villager, then right-click a tree, berry bush, or gold deposit.

BUILD

Use the build commands, then click a clear spot near your settlement.

ATTACK

Select soldiers and right-click an enemy or their stronghold. Keep villagers working to fund your army.

TIP   Select a unit near your Town Center to train villagers, soldiers, or archers. Houses increase your population limit. Enemy raids grow stronger over time.
